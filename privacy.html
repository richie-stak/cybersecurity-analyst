Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Cybersecurity Analyst plugin is designed to handle information. It applies to the skills-based plugin published by Richard Adjei | Cyber Security Engineer (the “Publisher”).

1. How the plugin works

Cybersecurity Analyst is a skills-based instruction package used within ChatGPT. It does not operate its own hosted application, independent database, analytics service or plugin backend. It does not independently collect, store, transmit or sell user information.

2. Conversations and files

When you use the plugin, the information you choose to enter, including prompts, security logs, reports, configuration details or uploaded files, is processed by ChatGPT in accordance with OpenAI’s applicable terms, privacy policy and product settings. The Publisher does not receive access to your ChatGPT conversations or uploaded files through this plugin and does not independently retain copies of them.

Do not submit passwords, private keys, access tokens, personal data or confidential organisational information unless you are authorised to do so and have assessed the applicable data-handling requirements. Where possible, redact or minimise sensitive information before sharing it.

3. Information received by the Publisher

If you contact the Publisher by email, the Publisher will receive the information you include in that message, such as your email address and the contents of your support request. That information will be used to respond to the request and will not be collected through the plugin itself.

4. Third parties and external services

The plugin does not integrate its own external analytics, advertising, telemetry or data-processing services. ChatGPT and its underlying service providers may process information as described in OpenAI’s own policies.

5. Data retention and your choices

The Publisher does not maintain a separate store of plugin conversations or uploaded files. For information about ChatGPT conversation retention, data controls, export or deletion, consult OpenAI’s current privacy documentation and settings.

6. Children

This plugin is intended for use by people who are permitted to use ChatGPT under the applicable service terms. It is not designed specifically for children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect material changes to the plugin or its data practices. The revised date will be shown at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about the plugin, contact richadjei@gmail.com.

This policy describes the plugin’s current skills-only design. If the plugin later adds a backend, integrations, analytics or other data collection, this policy must be reviewed and updated before those features are released.
