Cybersecurity Analyst is a structured AI assistant that helps security professionals investigate issues, analyse evidence, assess risk, plan defensive response and produce clear professional reports.

Explore capabilitiesGet support
Designed for defensive use. Use it only on systems, networks and information you own or are explicitly authorised to assess.
What it helps with

Structured support across the security lifecycle

Bring relevant context and evidence into your conversation and use the assistant to organise analysis and next steps.

01

Security analysis

Review security events, logs and technical observations; identify patterns, explain likely significance and highlight evidence gaps.

02

Threat intelligence

Structure threat information, assess relevance to an environment and translate findings into practical defensive considerations.

03

Incident response

Support triage, investigation planning, containment considerations, recovery planning and post-incident lessons.

04

Vulnerability assessment

Help interpret findings, assess risk, prioritise remediation and prepare clear vulnerability assessment reports.

05

Security governance

Support security architecture discussions, control reviews, risk documentation and governance or compliance reporting.

06

Professional reporting

Turn validated observations into concise, structured reports with impact, evidence, recommendations and limitations.

A practical assistant, not an authority

Keep human judgement in the loop.

AI-generated analysis can be incomplete or incorrect. Validate findings against original evidence, trusted sources, your organisation's procedures and qualified professional judgement. The assistant does not replace incident command, legal advice, an independent penetration test or a formal audit.
